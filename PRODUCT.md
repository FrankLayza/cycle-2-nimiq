# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile Nimiq Pay users seeking a quick arcade match, friends joining a private 1v1 by room code, daily competitors pursuing verified scores, and competition judges evaluating the wallet integration and trust model.

## Product Purpose

Competitive Snake provides 60 to 90 second skill-based matches with server-authoritative outcomes. Wallet identity, deterministic replay verification, daily competition, and team-funded NIM rewards make trust visible without requiring players to deposit funds.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, energetic, trustworthy. The experience should feel like a polished mobile arcade game inside a payment app, with clear competitive state and minimal setup friction.

## Anti-references

Avoid neon crypto styling, dark speculative-finance aesthetics, generic dashboard layouts, placeholder geometric game art, deceptive wallet prompts, and decorative effects that obscure gameplay state.

## Design Principles

- Put the player into a match quickly; wallet and reward features must not block free play.
- Make authoritative match state legible at a glance, especially scores, countdown, survival, and connection status.
- Keep rendering downstream of the deterministic simulation and server state.
- Use playful presentation without compromising touch accuracy, performance, or trust.
- Treat mobile WebView constraints, reduced motion, and non-color cues as core product requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast, at least 44px touch targets, keyboard support in desktop browsers, non-color-only gameplay cues, and reduced-motion behavior. Controls and status text must remain usable inside the rotated mobile viewport and safe areas.
